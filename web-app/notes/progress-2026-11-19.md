### What was wrong with sharing

- The share image was WordPress's featured image. Two of the first 100 listings had a **PDF** there, so no preview. One had none.
- About **1 in 5 images were over 300 KB** (some 3-4 MB). WhatsApp tends to skip big images.
- Text came straight from WordPress, so previews showed `&amp;` and the like.
- A page that sets its own `openGraph` drops the site-wide image, so pages without their own image had none at all. The site default also used a relative path.
- Quote pages had no share data at all, so a shared quote showed only "Zimbabwhere".
- The X and email share buttons put the raw title in the link, so a name with `&` cut the link short.

What it does now: clean text (entities and HTML removed, "NULL" ignored, cut at a word boundary), the logo or featured image only if it really is an image, resized to 750px wide through `/_next/image` (all 97 images I tried loaded, over-300 KB went from 18 to 1), and the Zimbabwhere card when there's no usable image. Share buttons encode their links properly.

Still large after resizing: `john-mahove-fine-art` (3 MB PNG screenshot, 507 KB after resize).

### International listings

File: `notes/international-listings-review.csv` (1,274 rows). It is in `.gitignore`, so it won't be committed.
Open it in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Each row has a link to the listing, a link to its edit screen in WordPress, and a map link.

- **High (846):** a foreign phone prefix with no +263 number anywhere, or a map pin / province outside Zimbabwe, or a foreign website and email, or a US-style number plus a foreign website or place name. Review quickly, most are clearly foreign.
- **Medium (361):** one weaker sign, mainly a 10-digit number with no country code (looks US) or a foreign website.
- **Low (67):** a +263 number as well as a foreign one (e.g. Zimbabwean company with a South African WhatsApp), or only a place name in the title. Many of these are probably fine.
- 4 rows have "Zimbabwe" in the name (Caparol Zimbabwe, Zimbabwe Lphp Courses, Easigrass Zimbabwe, Sabips Designs Zimbabwe). They are marked "double-check".
- Mostly USA/Canada (~600), UK, Australia, India, UAE. Only ~17 South Africa.
- Webula (7651) and Webula Test (10453) are left out on purpose.
- The `country` field is useless (all "Zimbabwe") and a map pin inside Zimbabwe proves nothing, so neither is used.
- Can't catch: a foreign business with a local-looking `0xx` number and no website.

### Review page in the WordPress dashboard

Built as a plugin: `wp-content/plugins/zw-international-review/` (`zw-international-review.php` + `data.php`, 1,274 listings).
Upload the folder by FTP, then Plugins, Activate. A new "International listings" item appears in the left menu (admins only).

- Tabs: To review, Drafted / removed, Kept. Filter by confidence, search by name, country or phone. 50 per page.
- Each row: **View** (site), **Edit** (WordPress), **Draft** and **Keep** buttons. Tick boxes + "Move ticked to draft" for bulk (asks to confirm, max 100 per click).
- Draft = status set to draft. Nothing is deleted. Republish from the listing's Edit screen.
- Not draftable from here: a paid premium listing (it warns), and the Webula test listings (not in the list at all).
- After drafting, press "Refresh search suggestions". The site's search suggestions come from a saved file (`json/business_search.json`) that is only rewritten when that feed is opened, so drafted names would otherwise stay in the suggestions.
- The list is a snapshot from 19 Nov (the page shows each listing's live status). Listings added later won't be in it.
- Delete the plugin when the clean-up is done.

Checked: PHP syntax on 7.4, 8.3 and 8.5, and a fake-WordPress test harness (render, filters, paging, draft, keep, premium and test-listing protection, 100 cap, token and admin checks). **Not run inside a real WordPress**: the local MySQL (MAMP PRO) is off. Try one listing first.
